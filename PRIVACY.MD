# Privacy Policy — ShieldTracker DeFi

**Effective:** 31/07/2026
**Last updated:** 31/07/2026
**Publisher:** ShieldTracker
**Contact:** support@shieldtrackerdefi.com

ShieldTracker DeFi does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. Every calculation runs inside your browser, on your device, and never leaves it.

---

## 1. What the extension does

ShieldTracker DeFi is a calculator. You enter a buy price, a sell price, an amount held, and optional buy and sell fees. The extension multiplies and subtracts those numbers and shows you the amount invested, the amount returned, the profit or loss, and the ROI. That is its single purpose, and it does nothing outside of it.

## 2. Data we handle

| Category | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| Personally identifiable information (name, address, email, age, ID numbers) | Not collected |
| Health information | Not collected |
| Financial and payment information | Not collected |
| Authentication information (passwords, credentials, tokens) | Not collected |
| Personal communications (email, messages) | Not collected |
| Location | Not collected |
| Web history | Not collected |
| User activity (clicks, keystrokes, mouse position, analytics) | Not collected |
| Website content (text, images, page data) | Not collected |
| Numbers you type into the calculator | Stays on device |

The prices, amounts, and fee percentages you type are held in the extension's popup only for as long as it takes to display a result. They are not linked to you, not sent anywhere, and not retained by the publisher in any form.

## 3. Local storage

If the extension saves your last entered values so they are still there when you reopen the popup, that data is written to your browser's local extension storage on your own computer. It is not synced to us and not readable by any website. You can clear it at any time by using the Reset button or by removing the extension, which deletes its storage with it.

## 4. Network activity

> The extension makes no network requests. It does not fetch live prices, does not load remote code, fonts, or images, and does not contain analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking pixels of any kind. Every file it runs is bundled in the package you install.

## 5. Permissions

The extension requests only the permissions listed on its Chrome Web Store page, and only what its calculator function requires. It does not request access to your browsing history, your tabs, your bookmarks, or the content of the pages you visit.

## 6. Third parties

There are no third parties. We do not use external services, SDKs, or hosting providers to process anything you enter, because nothing you enter is ever processed anywhere but your own machine. We do not sell or transfer user data to anyone, we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

## 7. This website

[shieldtrackerdefi.com](https://shieldtrackerdefi.com) is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub records standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention when a page is requested; that is GitHub's processing, described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), and we have no access to those logs.

## 8. Children

The extension is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

## 9. Your rights
